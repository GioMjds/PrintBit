# Code of Conduct

## PrintBit — Coin-Operated Self-Service Printing Kiosk

---

## Our Pledge

We, the contributors and maintainers of PrintBit, pledge to make participation
in this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for
everyone — regardless of age, background, level of experience, education,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or gender identity and
expression.

We are a small capstone team, but we hold ourselves to the same standard as
any professional open-source community. We build in good faith, give feedback
constructively, and treat every contributor's time and effort as valuable.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language in issues, pull requests, and comments.
- Give and receive constructive code review feedback graciously.
- Respect differing technical opinions — debate ideas, not people.
- Acknowledge the work of others; credit collaborators appropriately.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its end users (campus students
  and staff who rely on the kiosk).
- Report bugs and security vulnerabilities responsibly (see `SECURITY.md`).
- Keep discussions on-topic and relevant to the project.

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information (e.g., real names, contact details)
  without explicit consent.
- Trolling, unconstructive criticism, or deliberate derailment of discussions.
- Dismissing contributions without explanation or justification.
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional or inappropriate in a
  collaborative academic or workplace setting.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews,
  commit messages).
- Any communication channels used by the team (group chats, emails, etc.).
- Public representation of the project at academic presentations, demos,
  or conferences.

---

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behavior and are expected to take fair, corrective action in response to any
conduct they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits,
issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct,
and to temporarily or permanently block contributors whose behavior is deemed
harmful.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by opening a **[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/GioMjds/printbit/security/advisories/new)**
for private reporting, or by contacting the lead developer directly via their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/GioMjds).

All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated with discretion.
Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter.

Depending on the severity and context, enforcement actions may include:

| Severity              | Action                                                       |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Minor / first offense | Private written warning with clarification of the violation. |
| Repeated or moderate  | Temporary suspension from contributing or commenting.        |
| Severe / sustained    | Permanent ban from all project spaces.                       |

---

## Academic Integrity

As a capstone project, PrintBit is also subject to the academic integrity
policies of our institution. Contributors must not:

- Plagiarize code, documentation, or ideas from other projects without
  proper attribution.
- Misrepresent the contributions of any team member.
- Submit PrintBit work as their individual effort when it was collaborative,
  or vice versa.

Violations of academic integrity will be handled in accordance with
institutional policy in addition to the enforcement actions above.
